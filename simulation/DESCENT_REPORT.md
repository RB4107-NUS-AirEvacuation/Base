# Owner DESCEND simulation — exploratory

Five CAD-based eight-station cases, 60 s app limit; a separate 1 ms-step diagnostic continues the descents until the second DONE. Robot nose faces downhill for both directions. The owner-supplied L12 level length is 111 mm; spatial tendon range 82–112 mm. Same CAD pin approximations and nose-down hinge as previous model. No change to CAD masses. See `owner_extended_results.json`, `owner_controller.py`, `owner_*_*.xml` and `owner_*.gif`.

At every angle the first CRUISE times out (never flat again), second APPROACH times out (no edge found), and second CRUISE times out (never flat again). First command ends on stairs; second passes the bottom without stopping and ultimately stops about 1.53, 1.61, 1.66 m beyond the world origin, respectively, far past the first riser at x=0.345 m. The actual 60 s app run ends in second APPROACH: a position goal there does not prove the robot stopped. This is a state-machine/geometry model result, not safe operational advice.

Local first 60 s worst chair tilt from level: down 24° 7.99°, 32° 17.86°, 40° 33.67°; up 24° 14.04°, 40° 3.21°. Both ascent cases fail to reach the landing within 60 s; 40° stalls at the first riser, and 24° is still on the stairs. At 60 s measured x/z (m) are (0.658,0.182) at 24° and (0.143,0.042) at 40°. These are sampled at 10 ms. The 1 ms and preliminary 2 ms results differed significantly: contact/timestep convergence is not established, so exact numbers are provisional. Motor and load assumptions are also significant.

The controller ports the reference state transitions and timeout-advance rules, speeds, range sampling, 15 Hz acceleration low pass and PWM slew. Range uses an ideal narrow ray from an estimated base-tray front-edge location, with 1 m maximum; pitch and encoder data are ideal. Acceleration is derived by differencing base-origin velocity at 100 Hz; Hall triggering is idealized to 3 samples at 0.005 rad from straight. Encoder velocity is a 20 ms moving difference with alpha 0.5. The PI speed loop uses app_config gains; the modeled zero-speed command clears the PI and leaves back-EMF damping, an assumption in the absence of the channel implementation. There is no port of TwoMotorSync heading-hold code; independent PI tracks do not demonstrate R4 5% synchronization. Actuator 50 N/10 mm/s, hinge 2.45 Nm/80 rpm and track external ratio are unverified estimates, not installed variants. Hall and down-end physical stops/clearance are not verified. Tendon constraints are compliant and may numerically penetrate; the requested setpoint is clamped to the stroke.

Not a safety case: continuous attitude is assumed; E-stop, lost/stale IMU, drive isolation, hung controller, power loss, independent loaded-chair retention, track brakes, 60-second hold, independent catch and exclusion zone are not demonstrated. No electrical/invoice/startup or physical stair adjustment validation. The eight-step stair is shorter than the full specified height. Robot CAD mass is about 7.4 kg before additions, not 3 kg; materials and solid envelopes require audit and actual weighing. Dummy mass 250 g and rigid restraint are assumptions. The imported visible meshes are non-colliding; roller/cleat contacts approximate flexible belts, and L12 physical linkage/actuator housing is simplified. Thus no prediction should substitute for testing with a qualified independent slack catch, secured dummy and exclusion zone.

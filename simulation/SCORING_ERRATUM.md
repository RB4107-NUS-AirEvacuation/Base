# Submission scoring erratum

The app's reach-goal measurements are displacement from the initial position, not absolute world coordinates. The submitted reach thresholds were mistakenly specified as world coordinates. Consequently all ten reach-goal pass/fail flags in `_results/harness.json` are invalid; the submitted overall count of 5/20 cannot be interpreted as the machine's score. No second harness run was made because the owner stated that this was the last budgeted run.

World-position diagnostic values and state logs in OWNER_DESCENT_REPORT.md remain valid for the same 1 ms local test. At 60 s all three descent models are at the bottom plane, but are still driving in their second APPROACH; they therefore do not show a completed stop at the bottom. Both ascent models fail to reach the landing by 60 s. For a future app rerun, the corresponding 24°/32°/40° descent upper displacement thresholds on x are -863.7/-836.2/-801.7 mm and on z approximately -189.2/-248.4/-302.5 mm, subject to confirming how negative upper limits are represented. The 24°/40° ascent minimum x displacements are 853.7/791.7 mm; minimum z displacements are 191.2/304.5 mm.

The app does correctly report the chair-tilt results and hinge deviations: descent maxima 8.0°, 17.9°, 33.7°; ascent 14.0° and 3.2°. Hinge worst deviation from straight during ascent: 12.1° at 24° (fails the 2° allowance) and 0.1° at 40°. The latter has not progressed beyond the first riser. Hinge range is compliant within the modeled numerical tolerance in descents, but an end-position goal does not prove the required transition sequence.
